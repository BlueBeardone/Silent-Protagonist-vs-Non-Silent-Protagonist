Privacy Policy

This is a small research project comparing how people feel about silent versus non-silent protagonists. This page explains what the Community tab collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used to identify the comment and to follow up occasionally for the research.
  • Your comment — free text you write.
  • The time the comment was posted.
  • Your IP address — used only to rate-limit requests and to stop spam. It is not stored in the comment collection.

What we show

Comments are public. The email address is masked before it is shown (for example bar***@gmail.com); the full address is never returned to a browser.

Why we collect it

The email lets us follow up with people who want to take part in the research. The comment is the research data. The IP is only used to keep the endpoint usable.

How long we keep it

Comments are kept for the research project. The operator can set a retention period with the RETENTION_DAYS environment variable and delete older comments. The default is to keep them until the project ends.

Deleting your data

To have your comment and email removed, contact the operator and include the email address you used (or the comment text). The admin can delete a comment by id with the DELETE /api/comments/<id> endpoint.

Third parties

The app is hosted by the operator. The comment database is hosted by MongoDB Atlas. The live player numbers come from Steam and SteamCharts. If spam protection is enabled, the site uses Cloudflare Turnstile.

Cookies

This app does not set cookies. The Flutter web app may use local storage for its own preferences; the comments API does not use cookies.
